{\itshape How much of hazard news is news? Decomposing open-source coverage of chemical accidents and CBRN threats for hazard surveillance}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=4pt]
\item News volume for 30 hazard queries is decomposed into five labelled components
\item Human coders put the CBRN event share at 5\%: one article in twenty
\item Reference bias is 33\% for hydrogen fluoride and 89\% for Novichok, 1--2\% in controls
\item Article counts for six substance queries are unrelated to official incident counts
\item The gap survives excluding off-topic hits: 14\% of on-topic CBRN news is event
\end{itemize}
